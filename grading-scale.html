Grading Scale

GradeTrack SL converts each letter grade into grade points, then weighs those points by credit hours to compute your GPA. The table below is the scale used throughout this tool.

Reference Scale

Letter Grade Grade Points Typical Percentage Band
This is a standard Commonwealth-style reference scale, not an official copy of any single institution's handbook. Grade point values can differ slightly between universities and even between programmes. If your institution's official scale uses different decimal values, your true GPA may vary slightly from what this calculator shows. Use the Feedback page to report your institution's exact scale so it can be considered for a future update.

How the Formula Works

For each course, grade points are multiplied by credit hours to get quality points. The semester GPA is the sum of all quality points divided by the total credit hours attempted that semester.

GPA = (Σ grade points × credit hours) ÷ (Σ credit hours)
