About

Why GradeTrack SL exists

The Challenge

Calculating academic performance can often feel complex and confusing. With credit-weighted formulas, varying grade point values, and different thresholds for academic standing, many students find it challenging to calculate their GPA accurately by hand or keep a clear track of their progress.

Understanding how individual course credits impact overall standings is essential for academic success, yet interpreting raw numbers without clear guidance can make it difficult to know exactly where your semester stands.

The Approach

GradeTrack SL keeps the focus narrow: a single, reliable credit-weighted GPA calculator, a transparent reference grading scale, a private history of calculated semesters, and a feedback channel for students to report their own institution's exact scale. Nothing is sent to a server — all history is stored locally in the student's own browser.

Built With

HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks, no build step, deployed as a static site via GitHub Pages.
